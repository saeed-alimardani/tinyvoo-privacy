Privacy Policy — Shark Splash!

Effective date: October 1, 2026 · Last updated: October 1, 2026

Shark Splash! is a game for children. We respect children's privacy.

We do not collect, store or share any personal information.

  • No personal data collection — none at all.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no third-party data-collecting SDKs.
  • No advertisements, no in-app purchases, no accounts, no sign-in.
  • The game works fully offline. It has no network permission and makes no network communication.
  • Game progress (levels, stars, best score, chosen character, settings) is saved only on your device in the app's local storage. It never leaves the device and is removed when the app is uninstalled.
  • The device vibration is used only for short, optional game feedback and can be turned off in the game's Settings.
